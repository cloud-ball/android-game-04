# Course structure and player progression in well-rated mobile and casual golf games

Research date: 2026-11-14. Researched for Cup Dash, an original offline golf-vs-CPU game (Playjar, Godot 4.7, Android portrait). The games below are named only as sources. Mechanics are described generically. Do not copy course names, tour names or distinctive designs.

Source caveat: many pages were read through search-result summaries or a fetch summariser rather than in full. Where sources disagree, both are noted. Several listings (App Store) change over time, so counts reflect the date they were read.

## 1. How many courses and holes do good golf games ship, and how many holes per course on mobile?

### Takeaway
Course counts vary widely: about 5 at launch for a console arcade golf game, about 20 nine-hole courses for a premium-style mobile golf game, and 50+ courses or 100+ levels for long-running or puzzle titles. On mobile and in casual modes, the unit of play is almost always short: 1-hole duels, 3-hole matches, or 9-hole rounds. 18 holes is mainly a console or "full round" option. Reviewers penalise thin launch rosters (5 courses was called "slim"). Games grow by adding courses in free updates.

### Cited Findings
**Mobile, premium or free-to-play with permanent unlocks**
- Side-on 2D mobile golf (Super Stickman Golf 3, 2016): 20 courses of 9 holes each, about 180 holes. Common Sense Media describes single player as 5 tours × 4 courses × 9 holes. — [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/super-stickman-golf-3); [TapSmart review](https://www.tapsmart.com/games/review-super-stickman-golf-3/); [Pocket Gamer review](https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/070661/super-stickman-golf-3-review-whos-your-caddy/)
  - A Stuff launch review said only 5 courses were available at that point, which suggests the roster was staged or rolled out after launch. — [Stuff](https://stuff.tv/?p=85057). Gamepressure claims one course has "200+ holes", which conflicts with the 9-holes-per-course structure and is probably an error. — [Gamepressure](https://gamepressure.com/games/super-stickman-golf-3/z14b74)
- Pixel-art mobile golf with a career mode (Pixel Pro Golf, iOS): "over 50 courses", "over 800 holes" (about 16 holes per course on average). Online play uses random 3- or 9-hole layouts. Rated 4.6/5 from 452 ratings. New courses, alternate pin positions and practice holes were added in updates through 2024-2025. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/pixel-pro-golf/id1534171570) (read 2026-11)
- Cartoon 3D golf on Apple Arcade (Clap Hanz Golf, April 2021): "6+" themed world courses. Rounds are 3, 6 or 9 holes. Score Attack uses 9-hole rounds. 20+ characters to recruit. — [SuperPhillip Central review, Apr 2021](https://www.superphillipcentral.com/2021/04/clap-hanz-golf-ios-review.html); [Gematsu, Apr 2021](https://www.gematsu.com/2021/04/clap-hanz-golf-now-available-for-apple-arcade)
- Mini-golf battler (Golf Battle): App Store copy says "120+ holes, courses and levels". Matches have up to 6 players, in a classic round or a "Rush" (fastest to the hole) mode. — [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/golf-battle); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/golf-battle/id1422866002)

**Mobile, free-to-play PvP (structure reference only)**
- 3D PvP golf (Golf Clash): a match is a single hole, 1v1. A tie (described as very common) goes to a one-shot "closest to the pin" shootout. The App Store listed "13 challenging tours", and higher tours bring "more challenging course conditions". Each tour holds a handful of courses, which rotate by season. Fan guides describe 9 holes per tour course. EA's Season 74 rotation shows Tour 13 with 5 courses. — [Pocket Gamer "three things"](https://www.pocketgamer.com/golf-clash/golf-clash-three-things-to-know-about-the-incredibly-popular-golf-game); [AndroidGuys review](https://androidguys.com/reviews/golf-clash-is-it-good-enough-to-be-the-leader-in-the-clubhouse-review/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/golf-clash/id1089225191); [West Games Tour 8 guide](https://west-games.com/golf-clash-tour-8/); [EA Season 74 rotation](https://ea.com/en-gb/games/golf-clash/news/season-74-course-rotation)
- The same game runs a separate "9-Hole Cup" tournament: 3 days long, with each round 9 holes. — [Playdemic help](https://playdemic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-golf-clash/faq/1144-what-are-9-hole-cup-tournaments)
- Another arcade golf game (Easy Come Easy Golf) uses 3-hole online matches for up to 4 players. — cited in search summary of [Digitally Downloaded review](https://www.digitallydownloaded.net/2022/09/review-easy-come-easy-golf-nintendo-switch.html)

**Console and PC casual golf**
- Kart-style party golf (Mario Golf: Super Rush, Switch, launched 25 Jun 2021): 6 standard 18-hole courses at launch (all par 72), plus a battle stadium with two 9-hole layouts. Free updates added 1 course (v2.0, 5 Aug 2021), 2 courses (v3.0, 23 Sep 2021) and 2 more (v4.0, 23 Nov 2021), for 11 regular 18-hole courses. Two of the added courses are all-par-3 (par 54) with Amateur and Pro layouts. — [Super Mario Wiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Golf:_Super_Rush)
- Arcade sim golf (Everybody's Golf, PS4, Aug 2017): 5 courses on disc, with 2-3 more as paid DLC (sources disagree on the DLC count). A reviewer called "only five courses out of the box" slim. — [Press Start](https://press-start.com.au/reviews/playstation4-reviews/2017/08/29/everybodys-golf-review-old-friend-returns/); [SuperPhillip Central](https://superphillipcentral.com/2018/04/everybodys-golf-ps4-review.html?m=0); [Outcyders](https://www.outcyders.net/review/everybodys-golf-review-four-player-fun)
- Golf RPG (Golf Story, Switch, 2017): 8 real courses of 9 holes each, plus 3 unlockable retro "mini-game" courses (11 by one count). Story is 15-20 hours. A Quick Play mode opens the courses unlocked in the story. — [Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/games/golf_story/b/switch/archive/2017/09/30/nothing-short-of-charming.aspx); [Player One](https://www.player.one/golf-story-review-everything-solved-well-timed-swing-120497); [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/switch-eshop/golf_story); [Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/?p=223278)
- Golf puzzle (Golf Peaks): about 108-109 levels in 9 worlds of 12 levels each, with 3 bonus levels per world (counts vary by source). — [GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/a-puzzle-thats-par-for-the-course-golf-peaks-review/); [PowerUp Gaming, May 2019](https://powerup-gaming.com/2019/05/23/golf-peaks-switch-review-puzzling-putting)
- Physics-comedy golf (What the Golf?, Apple Arcade): a level-based world map with themed worlds. A free "A Hole New World" update added about 1,000 holes and 50 levels, and a later update added 30 more levels. 98% critic recommend on OpenCritic. — [Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/?p=274833); [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/083522/what-the-golf-gets-a-sporty-sports-update-that-adds-30-new-levels/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/What_the_Golf)

### Inferences
- The common mobile course size is **9 holes**: Super Stickman Golf 3 courses, Golf Story courses, Clap Hanz Golf's maximum round length, and Golf Clash tour courses. 18 holes is a console norm and seems too long for a portrait phone session.
- The common match unit is **1 hole (PvP duel) or 3 holes (quick match)**. That suits Cup Dash: 9-hole courses, played as quick 3-hole matches against a CPU, with the full 9 as a "round" or championship.
- A launch roster of about 5-6 courses works if each one feels distinct, but "5 courses" got "slim" complaints on console. With 9 holes each, 6 courses gives 54 holes. That is close to the Super Stickman Golf 3 launch state and far below its full roster.
- Variant layouts (Amateur/Pro, mirrored, alternate pins) are a cheap way to multiply content. Mario Golf: Super Rush, Pixel Pro Golf and Clap Hanz Golf all use them.

### Gaps
- No reliable source gave Golf Rival's or Golf Battle's holes per match, or the current (2026) number of Golf Clash tours. The App Store "13 tours" figure may be dated, and later seasons go past Tour 13.
- No source gave a precise Clap Hanz Golf course count beyond "6+".

## 2. How are courses unlocked, what feels rewarding vs grindy, and how long are sessions?

### Takeaway
Unlock methods fall into four families: (a) finish or progress a career or story mode, (b) beat a rival or boss CPU to raise a rank, (c) earn stars or points from events, and (d) PvP trophies with coin entry fees. Most praised: gates that come from playing well, offer parallel routes, and open quickly. Most criticised: slow XP or rank gates (one reviewer was still on the first course after 15 hours), coin-wager tours that drain balances, and replaying the same courses to level up.

### Cited Findings
- **Parallel route (praised design):** in Mario Golf: Super Rush, each standard course unlocks either through story-mode progress or by finishing 18 holes on the previous course in free play. The chain is linear: course 2 leads to 3, 3 to 4, and so on. — [Super Mario Wiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Golf:_Super_Rush)
- **Story structure:** in the same game, the story mode uses 3-hole practice rounds, 9-hole qualifiers and 18-hole challenges, and takes about 8 hours. — [Super Mario Wiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Golf:_Super_Rush); [search summary of guides](https://www.gameskinny.com/tips/mario-golf-super-rush-courses-guide-how-to-unlock-all-courses/)
- **Stars, rank and world map:** in Clap Hanz Golf, you earn stars from tournament events on a world map, which raises your rank. Higher ranks unlock courses, characters and harder, better-paying events. Match-play events against one CPU recruit that CPU as a playable character if you win. — [SuperPhillip Central](https://www.superphillipcentral.com/2021/04/clap-hanz-golf-ios-review.html)
  - Digitally Downloaded and Pocket Gamer both mention a grind, even with no microtransactions. — [Digitally Downloaded mini-review](https://www.digitallydownloaded.net/?p=41250); [Pocket Gamer review](https://ee.pocketgamer.com/articles/084131/clap-hanz-golf-review-having-a-ball/)
- **Rival or boss ladder:** in Everybody's Golf (PS4), each rank has 3 VS characters. Beating the boss raises your rank and unlocks courses. Reviewers called it "slightly too grindy" (Destructoid). Another reviewer was still limited to the first course after about 15 hours. — [Outcyders](https://www.outcyders.net/review/everybodys-golf-review-four-player-fun); [Well Played](https://www.well-played.com.au/everybodys-golf-review/); [Destructoid review in progress](https://vip-develop.destructoid.com/?p=204364)
- **Ranking points:** in Pixel Pro Golf's career, ranking points move you up the world rankings and unlock tours, ending at multi-day "Majors" scored over 4 rounds. Prize money buys equipment. A reviewer noted that prize money becomes worthless once the best gear is unlocked. Another dropped their rating from 4 stars to about 2 because the final course was too hard (a difficulty spike at the end). — [App Store listing and reviews](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/pixel-pro-golf/id1534171570)
- **Trophies plus coin wagers (PvP):** in Golf Clash, trophies set your tour. Beginner tours cap at 20 trophies, so you are pushed upward. Entry fees rise per tour (Tour 4 was 3,000 coins), and a win pays double. Rewards come in timed chests (2-8 hours, limited slots). Guides advise keeping 10× the entry fee in the bank. — [GameAnalytics blog (orig. 2018)](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/golfclash-swing-success); [Golf Clash Notebook FAQ](https://golfclashnotebook.io/faq); [Reddit bankroll guide](https://redlib.cypherpunktools.com/r/GolfClash/comments/bp9239/guide_bankroll_management_indepth_revisited)
  - The same game later added rewards for finishing a tour and per-tour "difficulty levels" (debuffs) that unlock as you win, for bonus rewards. — [EA 2.51 update notes](https://www.ea.com/games/golf-clash/news/golf-clash-2-51-0-update); [Playdemic help: tour difficulty levels](https://playdemic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-golf-clash/faq/1390-tour-difficulty-levels)
  - Complaints include coin wagering ("makes you gamble for coins") and matchmaking. These are user anecdotes. — [Common Sense Media: Golf Battle](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/golf-battle); [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/golf-battle/id1422866002)
- **Level-up by replay (criticised):** Golf Battle has you replay the same courses to level up and unlock the next. Common Sense Media flags it as pay-to-win with frustrating matchmaking. — [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/golf-battle)
- **Permanent unlocks praised:** TouchArcade's summary of Super Stickman Golf 3 says the permanent unlocks are "the way to go" in its free-to-play model, while Metacritic notes it "occasionally stumbles" in the new free-to-play mechanics. — search summaries of [TouchArcade](https://toucharcade.com/?p=198208) and [Metacritic](https://legacy.prod.metacritic.com/game/ios/super-stickman-golf-3)
- **Story gating:** Golf Story's Quick Play opens courses only as the story unlocks them. — [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/switch-eshop/golf_story)
- **Session length:** Golf Clash's single-hole match means "you'll be zipping through your rounds quickly". An analyst's example has a careful player fitting 3-5 matches into one session. — [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/golf-clash/golf-clash-three-things-to-know-about-the-incredibly-popular-golf-game); [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/golfclash-swing-success). Clap Hanz Golf lets you suspend a round at any time, which a reviewer praised for short sessions. — [SuperPhillip Central](https://www.superphillipcentral.com/2021/04/clap-hanz-golf-ios-review.html)

### Inferences
- For Cup Dash (offline, coin hub economy only), the best-reviewed pattern is: **unlock the next course by winning or clearing the previous course's CPU match or round, OR by earning N stars or medals across its holes**. That gives a skill route and a persistence route, like Mario Golf: Super Rush's dual path. Avoid making coins the only gate. Coin entry wagers drew "gambling" complaints and fit poorly with an offline game.
- Keep the first 2-3 unlocks quick, within the first sessions. The worst-reviewed case was a slow rank gate that held a player on course 1 for 15 hours.
- A "beat the rival CPU to recruit or unlock" beat is praised and suits vs-CPU play (Clap Hanz Golf, Everybody's Golf). It needs a fair, readable CPU. Clap Hanz Golf's CPU was judged too easy, and Everybody's Golf's rank grind too slow.
- Flatten the late-game spike, or make the last course optional or bonus. Pixel Pro Golf lost a review star over a too-hard final course.
- Session estimate (inferred, not measured): a single mobile hole takes roughly 0.5-1.5 minutes, so a 3-hole match runs about 3-5 minutes and 9 holes about 10-15 minutes. Allow saving mid-round.

### Gaps
- I found no published telemetry on minutes per hole or per round in mobile golf. The session figures above are inferred.
- I found no exact unlock numbers (stars per course) for Super Stickman Golf 3 or Clap Hanz Golf.

## 3. Offline single-player structures: career, challenges, dailies, rivals, records, medals

### Takeaway
Good single-player golf layers (1) a career or tour spine (world map or ranked tours, ending in a multi-round championship), (2) per-course replay goals (score attack with graded ranks or points, time attack), (3) skill mini-challenges (closest to the pin, long drive, target golf, putting), (4) survival or streak modes, and (5) a rotating daily or weekly event. Rivals, as match-play duels against named CPUs, are a recurring hook.

### Cited Findings
- **Tour on a world map with mixed event types** (Clap Hanz Golf): stroke-play tournaments award stars. Match play against a single CPU recruits that golfer if you win. Drive and closest-to-pin duels give each side 3 shots and compare averages, and reward cosmetics. Events change rules: mirrored courses, no wind, or bunkers and rough out of bounds. — [SuperPhillip Central](https://www.superphillipcentral.com/2021/04/clap-hanz-golf-ios-review.html)
- **Score Attack with graded rank per course** (Clap Hanz Golf): a 9-hole round on any unlocked course. Your score earns a rank worth up to 5 points per course, and point thresholds unlock cosmetics. A **Survival** mode strings holes together outside the campaign. Nintendo Life called Survival and Score Attack good for pick-up-and-play. — [SuperPhillip Central](https://www.superphillipcentral.com/2021/04/clap-hanz-golf-ios-review.html); [Pocket Gamer](https://ee.pocketgamer.com/articles/084131/clap-hanz-golf-review-having-a-ball/); [Nintendo Life (sister title)](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/switch-eshop/easy-come-easy-golf)
- **Weekly rotating event:** a "World Tournament" with rules that change weekly and a leaderboard. — search summary of [TouchArcade review](https://toucharcade.com/?p=282731)
- **Solo challenge suite** (Mario Golf: Super Rush): Score Attack, Time Attack and a one-on-one putting challenge, each over 18 holes. Target Golf (added v4.0) has 3 target maps (plain, forest with river, volcano with elevation). Solo Target Golf ends when you miss one target 3 times. — [Super Mario Wiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Golf:_Super_Rush)
- **Daily tournament with seasons** (Pixel Pro Golf): one attempt per day on a different course. 20 daily events make a season, and season points crown a champion. Open and Handicap leagues, where handicap gives weaker players extra shots. Leaderboards and achievements exist for every course. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/pixel-pro-golf/id1534171570). This one is online, but the structure can be adapted offline as a seeded daily course.
- **Career ending in Majors:** 4-round totals (Pixel Pro Golf). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/pixel-pro-golf/id1534171570)
- **Rival ladder:** 3 VS characters per rank, ending in a boss (Everybody's Golf). — [Outcyders](https://www.outcyders.net/review/everybodys-golf-review-four-player-fun)
- **Story with three big tournaments as act breaks** (Golf Story). — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/golf-story-review-nintendo-switchs-hole-in-one-blend-of-rpg-and-links/)
- **Optional per-level challenges** that players don't need to clear to finish (What the Golf?). Golf Peaks has 3 bonus levels per world. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/What_the_Golf); [PowerUp Gaming](https://powerup-gaming.com/2019/05/23/golf-peaks-switch-review-puzzling-putting)
- **Replay value complaint:** Golf Peaks gives "not much incentive to go back" after the last level, and a Metacritic critic called the package "a little on the light side". — [PowerUp Gaming](https://powerup-gaming.com/2019/05/23/golf-peaks-switch-review-puzzling-putting); [Metacritic](https://legacy.prod.metacritic.com/game/pc/golf-peaks)

### Inferences
- A suggested offline menu per course for Cup Dash:
  - **Match vs CPU** (3 holes; the unlock gate)
  - **Full round** (9 holes, stroke play vs CPU field, giving a course record)
  - **Score Attack** (9 holes solo, bronze/silver/gold medal or 1-3 stars versus par targets, plus a personal best)
  - **Skill challenge** (closest to the pin or targets, one shot type per course)
- Medals per hole, such as par for bronze, birdie for silver and eagle or a hole-in-one for gold, give holes replay value without new content.
- A "championship" (4 × 9 holes, or all courses back to back) gives a career finale, as Majors do.
- An offline daily: a seeded date (core/Rng.gd) picks course, hole set and wind, and the player gets one scored attempt for a local streak. This copies the "one attempt per day" hook without needing a server.
- Rule-modifier events (no wind, mirrored, out-of-bounds bunkers) multiply content cheaply. They are the strongest "more from the same holes" lever in the evidence.

### Gaps
- No source detailed how daily challenges work in offline-only golf games. Most dailies found are online-leaderboard based.

## 4. How do games make each course feel different beyond looks, and what is the difficulty curve?

### Takeaway
Courses differ by a signature mechanic or hazard (wind, weather, quicksand, moving hazards, bounce walls, elevation), by par mix (some courses are all par 3), and by condition tiers that rise with progression (stronger wind, tougher conditions on higher tours). Rule modifiers and variant layouts add difficulty within a course.

### Cited Findings
- Each course in Mario Golf: Super Rush has a signature hazard:
  - a windy lakeside course with cliffs and tornadoes that lift balls
  - a desert with dunes and quicksand
  - woods with heavy rain and thunderstorms
  - a snow course with strong crosswinds and blizzards that change ball roll
  - a dusk desert where rain shortens shot distance
  - an all-par-3 city where shots bounce off building walls
  - an all-par-3 sanctuary with flying creatures
  - a final course with moving hazards

  The order runs from a Rookie course to a hazard-heavy finale. — [Super Mario Wiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Golf:_Super_Rush)
- Par mix as differentiation: two of the 11 Super Rush courses are par 54 (all par 3) and the rest par 72. — [Super Mario Wiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Golf:_Super_Rush)
- Higher Golf Clash tours bring "more challenging course conditions". Wind is central (in the guide's pin-adjust method, one ring = 2 mph). Players complain when displayed wind and ball flight seem inconsistent, which shows that **wind readability** matters. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/golf-clash/id1089225191); [LevelWinner guide](https://www.levelwinner.com/golf-clash-ultimate-guide-13-tips-tricks-become-best-player/); [Trustpilot](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/golfclash.com) (anecdotal)
- Opt-in difficulty tiers per tour ("debuffs") pay bonus rewards. — [Playdemic help](https://playdemic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-golf-clash/faq/1390-tour-difficulty-levels)
- Event rule changes: mirrored courses, no wind, out-of-bounds bunkers or rough (Clap Hanz Golf). — [SuperPhillip Central](https://www.superphillipcentral.com/2021/04/clap-hanz-golf-ios-review.html)
- Pixel Pro Golf reviewers praise trees and water that make shot planning matter, and slope chevrons that read well on greens. They also complain the final course was too hard. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/pixel-pro-golf/id1534171570)
- Puzzle golf ramps steeply. Golf Peaks "can get very difficult towards the end", and a reviewer wanted a hint system. — [Cog Connected](https://cogconnected.com/review/golf-peaks-review/); [Nintendo World Report](https://www.nintendoworldreport.com/review/49966/golf-peaks-switch-review)
- Easy CPU undermines the ramp: in Clap Hanz Golf, the CPU often hit errant shots, and tournaments didn't need high scores even late in the game. — [SuperPhillip Central](https://www.superphillipcentral.com/2021/04/clap-hanz-golf-ios-review.html)

### Inferences
- Give each Cup Dash course one headline mechanic plus a distinct par mix. Suggested original archetypes, described generically:
  1. Gentle parkland (no or low wind, wide fairways, slow greens; tutorial)
  2. Coastal links (strong and gusty wind, firm run-out, pot bunkers)
  3. Desert canyon (forced carries over sand or chasms, elevation)
  4. Alpine or snow (big elevation drops, wind at height, slick fast greens)
  5. Tropical islands (water carries, island greens, short par-3-heavy course)
  6. Night or "challenge" course (narrow, all hazards combined; optional finale)
- Ramp four parameters across courses: wind range, green speed (stimp), hazard density or carry distance, and CPU skill. Introduce one new mechanic per course and do not stack all of them early.
- An all-par-3 course is a cheap, praised variety lever, and it suits portrait short sessions.
- Wind must read clearly (arrow plus number) and behave consistently. This matters more for a skill-based offline game, where unfairness can't be blamed on an opponent.

### Gaps
- No source gave numeric wind or green-speed values per course or tour in any game.

## 5. Monetisation-neutral lessons: what keeps players coming back to single-player golf

### Takeaway
Repeat play comes from: permanent unlocks earned by play, collectible characters or cosmetics tied to beating rivals, graded per-course scores and medals to improve, rotating rule or daily events, steady new courses in free updates, and short, suspendable sessions. Repeat play suffers from: thin rosters, slow grinds, currency that loses value at the end, wager drain, and no reason to replay after finishing.

### Cited Findings
- Free content updates extended the life of top-rated titles. Mario Golf: Super Rush grew from 6 to 11 courses in 5 months (2021). What the Golf? added about 1,000 holes, 50 levels and later 30 more. Pixel Pro Golf added courses, pin positions and practice holes in 2024-2025 updates. — [Super Mario Wiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Golf:_Super_Rush); [Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/?p=274833); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/pixel-pro-golf/id1534171570)
- Cosmetic unlocks from Score Attack point thresholds and from challenge duels (Clap Hanz Golf). Characters level up slowly and gain small distance boosts, and every team member gains XP. — [SuperPhillip Central](https://www.superphillipcentral.com/2021/04/clap-hanz-golf-ios-review.html)
- Lots of hats and costumes plus secrets are praised alongside 20 courses (Super Stickman Golf 3). — [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/070661/super-stickman-golf-3-review-whos-your-caddy/)
- Prize money becomes pointless once the best gear is bought (Pixel Pro Golf user review). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/pixel-pro-golf/id1534171570)
- Timed chests and coin scarcity act as session limiters in PvP golf. This is a monetisation device, noted here as something not to copy offline. — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/golfclash-swing-success)
- Thin roster and grind complaints (Everybody's Golf), and weak replay after completion (Golf Peaks). — [Press Start](https://press-start.com.au/reviews/playstation4-reviews/2017/08/29/everybodys-golf-review-old-friend-returns/); [PowerUp Gaming](https://powerup-gaming.com/2019/05/23/golf-peaks-switch-review-puzzling-putting)

### Inferences
- Coin sinks for Cup Dash should stay useful after the core unlocks: cosmetics (ball trails, outfits), course variants (Pro tees, mirrored), and practice or challenge packs. Avoid coin-gated stat gear, which loses value at the end and can feel pay-to-win even with soft currency.
- Keep a per-course trophy shelf: best score, medals per hole, CPU rival beaten, hole-in-one flag. It drives completionist replay at zero content cost.
- Plan a "free update" cadence of 1 new course plus rule events. It matches how the best-reviewed games sustained play.

### Gaps
- No retention or D1/D7 data was found for single-player golf titles specifically. The lessons come from reviews, not telemetry.
