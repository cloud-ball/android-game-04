# Mobile golf controls, aiming, power, spin, wind and camera (portrait, one thumb)

Research date: 2026-11-14. 15 searches/fetches. Most sources are reviews (2013-2022), fan guide sites and user reviews; Pocket Gamer and official help pages are the strongest. TouchArcade's review pages returned HTTP 403, so they were used only through search snippets. Very few hard numbers (meter speeds, dead zones, gesture lengths) are published. Where a number is missing it is listed under Gaps and **not** invented.

Commercial games named below are research sources only. Cup Dash should copy mechanics described generically, never a game's names, ring/needle artwork, UI layout, branded ball or club names, or mode names (see the IP notes in each Inferences block).

## 1. Swing input schemes: which are rated highest for accessibility and skill depth, and what do reviews complain about?

### Takeaway
Shipped mobile golf games use four main schemes: (a) pull back on the ball and release on a moving timing needle; (b) a two-stroke swipe, down then up, where length sets power and sideways drift sets curve; (c) a simple slingshot pull-back; (d) tap-to-start/tap-to-stop meters. The best-reviewed portrait games use (a) or (c), operated by dragging from the ball. Swipe swings get "love it or hate it" reviews, and inconsistent power on short shots and putts is the top complaint across all swipe games.

### Cited Findings
- **Pull back plus timing needle (portrait 1v1 game, Golf Clash).** Tap the take-shot button (bottom right), drag the ball back to an empty marker behind it and hold. An arrow then sweeps left and right; releasing at centre gives a "perfect" shot. The needle starts slow and speeds up as you progress — [West Games, controls explained](https://west-games.com/golf-clash-controls/) (undated fan guide).
- Needle speed is published per ball as "the number of swings the needle makes in 1 second (lower is slower)". Balls differ only when you overpower; every ball has the same needle speed on normally powered shots — [search snippet of golfclashnotebook.io ball guide](https://golfclashnotebook.io/balls) (fan site; exact values not retrieved).
- Pulling past the targeting circle overpowers the shot: the screen vibrates and the needle "moves wildly". This is a risk/reward power band beyond 100% — [West Games accuracy explainer, via search](https://west-games.com/golf-clash-accuracy/).
- Players must release "a little bit before the needle hits the centre", which shows input latency is part of the skill. One guide says there is no sound cue when the needle passes perfect, so players learn the rhythm instead — [search summary of West Games / Level Winner guides](https://west-games.com/?p=15074) (fan guides; treat as anecdotal).
- Accuracy is a separate stat. One guide says it does not change the meter, but the shot still varies inside the landing ring; another says lower accuracy makes perfect shots less effective. The two sources conflict — [West Games accuracy](https://west-games.com/golf-clash-accuracy/); [Playdemic FAQ](https://playdemic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-golf-clash/faq/1285-why-did-my-shot-not-land-where-i-expected-1613730444).
- The official FAQ: a perfect shot "means nothing" without the right power. Players can deliberately use an off-centre "Great" release to offset crosswind — [Playdemic FAQ: Why did my shot not land where I expected](https://playdemic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-golf-clash/faq/1285-why-did-my-shot-not-land-where-i-expected-1613730444); [Playdemic: Adding Perfect Power](https://playdemic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-golf-clash/faq/1287-golf-tip-adding-power).
- Guides note it is "much harder to tell how far back you need to pull" when curl is added at the same time. Stacking inputs on one gesture costs readability — [search summary, Golf Clash guides](https://www.levelwinner.com/golf-clash-ultimate-guide-13-tips-tricks-become-best-player/).
- **Two-stroke swipe swing (arcade golf, Clap Hanz Golf / Easy Come Easy Golf).** Slide a finger down the right side of the screen to build power, then slide back up to hit. Drifting left or right during the swing makes a draw or fade. The reviewer calls the swing "love it or hate it" (4/5 overall) — [Pocket Gamer review, 2022-07-13](https://ee.pocketgamer.com/articles/084131/clap-hanz-golf-review-having-a-ball/).
- Players are split on that swipe swing: one says flick controls feel inconsistent next to buttons, another calls them surprisingly responsive — [search summary of player reviews](https://www.iofreeonline.com/clanguard20/CLAP-HANZ%20GOLF.html) (low-authority aggregator).
- **Swipe down/up (Golden Tee Mobile).** Swipe down to draw the club back ("the further you swipe, the more power"), swipe up to launch. Tapping anywhere while the club is drawn back cancels the swing, which is a built-in guard against accidental shots. A Shot Feedback display shows power and then the direction of the swing gesture — [Golden Tee Mobile help, How to Play](https://gtm-help.itsgames.com/gtm-help/help/howtoplay). A Pocket Gamer tips piece says the scheme "works very well" — [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/golden-tee-mobile/golden-tee-mobile-hints-tips-to-give-you-the-upper-hand-on-the-golf-course/).
- **Hold, then swipe (King of the Course).** Tap and hold to start the swing, then swipe up to finish it — [search snippet, Pocket Gamer King of the Course](https://www.pocketgamer.com/king-of-the-course/review).
- **Tap-tap meter (Super Stickman Golf, side-on).** Pick an angle, tap to start the shot meter, tap again to stop it and swing — [148Apps review](https://www.148apps.com/reviews/super-stick-golf-review). Pocket Gamer called the sequel's controls "all but perfect" — [Pocket Gamer SSG2](https://www.pocketgamer.com/super-stickman-golf-2/review/).
- **Drag on ball and release (Golf Blitz, side-on, portrait).** "Press and drag on the golf ball to aim, then release to shoot" — [Pocket Gamer, 2019-05-02, 4.5/5](https://www.pocketgamer.com/golf-blitz/golf-blitz-review-some-of-the-best-multiplayer-that-mobile-gaming-has-to-offer/). TouchArcade framed it for fans of "single handed portrait focussed games" — [TouchArcade, 2019-05-01](https://toucharcade.com/2019/05/01/golf-blitz-free-download-worldwide/).
- **Choose club, angle and power ("hope for the best").** A 3D golf game compared to a pool-style aimer. Praised as a swipe interface that "feels like an extension of the phone" and as a "dive-in-and-out" mobile experience — [Pocket Gamer PGA Tour Golf Shootout review, 2019-04-19](https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/080252/pga-golf-tour-shootout-review-walks-the-fine-line-between-sim-and-arcade/).
- **Slingshot complaints.** OK Golf is "very simple pull-and-release", with a stronger power shot that needs timing. Its controls are "a bit imperfect", with "some shots wind[ing] up not going where I've intended" — [Pocket Gamer OK Golf review](https://www.pocketgamer.com/ok-golf/ok-golf-review-a-golf-game-thats-a-little-bit-more-than-okay/). Wikipedia summarises a review calling the controls "sticky", with inconsistent speed of the screen and projection lines — [Wikipedia: OK Golf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OK_Golf).
- A "pull back to go forward" reversed motion "isn't forgiving for younger or new players" — [Common Sense Media, Mini Touch Golf](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/mini-touch-golf).
- **Store-review complaints about inconsistent swipes.** One App Store reviewer called a swipe golf game the "most inconsistent" they had played and said wind affected a drive and a short chip the same way — [Golf Impact App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/golf-impact-real-golf-game/id1532689217). Another said swipe speed seemed to have "almost no effect", so short chips and putts were hard — [Golf Dreams listing](https://game-solver.com/golf-dreams/). A WGT reviewer said the strike meter "jumps all over the place" — [WGT Golf, Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wgt.android.golf&hl=en_US). (All single user reviews.)
- **Forum asks for skill depth.** In a Pro Feel Golf thread, one commenter praised getting direction and spin "with only two touches"; another asked for a 3-press option or refined swipe to get backspin and fades — [TouchArcade forum thread](https://toucharcade.com/community/threads/254536).

### Inferences
- For one thumb in portrait, the strongest-evidenced scheme is **drag back from the ball, then release, with power shown as pull distance plus a release-timing accuracy element**. It runs the full 3D golf genre's most successful portrait game and the best-reviewed side-on portrait game. It is a single gesture, so the thumb never leaves the lower screen.
- Pure swipe-speed power has the worst reputation for consistency, especially for chips and putts. Map power to **pull distance**, not flick velocity. If a swipe swing is offered at all, make it optional.
- Skill depth comes from a timing element (needle or ring), an overpower band with an amplified error, and wind compensation. Using an off-centre timing release to counter wind is a deep emergent skill.
- Add a cancel affordance: drag back to the ball or into a dead zone, or tap elsewhere (as Golden Tee does). Accidental-shot complaints are common for drag schemes.
- IP: the concentric-ring target plus a left/right sweeping "needle" with a bullseye is a strongly associated trade-dress look. Cup Dash should present its timing element differently, for example a ring that shrinks or closes around the ball, an arc on the power bar, or a pulse, with its own art and words. Avoid "Perfect/Great" grading copied verbatim alongside rings.

### Gaps
- No published numbers for needle period at default speed, perfect-window width in ms or degrees, or swipe length to full power. The golfclashnotebook.io table lists swings per second per ball, but the page was not fetched.
- No published dead-zone sizes for drag-to-shoot. No Reddit thread directly comparing swipe and meter control was found.
- What the Golf, Golf Battle and Desert Golfing control reviews did not surface in searches.

## 2. Aiming: drag arrow vs landing target, club selection, expected carry, aim assists by difficulty

### Takeaway
Leading portrait games aim by **dragging a landing-target marker** on the course view, which shows where the ball first lands and how it bounces. A guide line whose length depends on equipment or skill acts as the aim assist. Club selection shows maximum distance, but games warn this is not an exact landing guide.

### Cited Findings
- Golf Clash: rings appear at the start of a shot to show where the ball first lands and how it bounces; you drag them to the landing spot. The concentric layers relate to accuracy, and the ring trajectory is "not always accurate, especially in later stages" — [West Games, controls](https://west-games.com/golf-clash-controls/).
- Club attributes include Power, Accuracy, Top Spin, Back Spin, Curl and Ball Guide. The length of the visible guide line is a stat that scales the aim assist — [search summary, West Games](https://west-games.com/golf-clash-controls/).
- Wind compensation works by shifting the aim point against the wind by a computed "adjustment" measured in rings — [search summary of a Golf Clash wind guide](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/112W2UYPWEqQBNKFv0ibJhwgFn4terlDRjBaBO_vm_9E/htmlpresent) (community slides, possibly outdated).
- Golden Tee Mobile: two-finger left/right swipe rotates the golfer to aim, double-tap re-centres. Club selection is tap, then swipe up/down. Max distance shows on the club but "isn't an exact landing guide", since slope, terrain, wind, loft, ball and power all matter — [GTM How to Play](https://gtm-help.itsgames.com/gtm-help/help/howtoplay).
- Clap Hanz Golf: moving the finger left or right shifts aim. Double-tap zooms to the aim point to check bunkers, water and distance. Near the flag, the power meter marks where the hole sits — [Pocket Gamer, 2022](https://ee.pocketgamer.com/articles/084131/clap-hanz-golf-review-having-a-ball/).
- Golf Blitz: aim and power come from one drag on the ball — [Pocket Gamer, 2019](https://www.pocketgamer.com/golf-blitz/golf-blitz-review-some-of-the-best-multiplayer-that-mobile-gaming-has-to-offer/).

### Inferences
- In portrait, a draggable landing marker on an overhead or high 3/4 view uses the tall screen well: the hole runs up the screen. The marker would sit in the upper half, which is a thumb stretch. So split it: **thumb drags the target anywhere on the course (or a relative drag from any point), and the shot gesture starts at the ball or a bottom zone.**
- Showing the **expected carry and roll (landing marker plus bounce dots)** with an imperfect ring for error matches the market. Scale the aim assist by difficulty, as the guide-line stat does: longer and more accurate preview on easy, shorter and blurrier on hard.
- Auto-select a club by distance and allow swipe or tap to change it, showing max carry per club as a label. Golden Tee's caveat shows the number should read as a nominal maximum.

### Gaps
- No source quantified how guide length or ring size scales with difficulty or equipment level.
- No portrait-specific study of where the thumb reaches on tall phones was found in this pass.

## 3. Spin control and putting / green reading

### Takeaway
Spin usually sits behind a separate ball-icon panel: tap a ball graphic and place the strike point for top, back or side spin, often unlocked later. Putting gets the most complaints for feel. Games handle it with putt range presets, a cutaway or grid slope view, and no curve on putts.

### Cited Findings
- Golf Clash: tap the ball icon (top right) to open an enlarged ball and set backspin, topspin and contact point. Spin is unavailable with starter equipment and unlocks with progression — [West Games](https://west-games.com/golf-clash-controls/). Curl into or against the wind is a control tool — [Playdemic FAQ](https://playdemic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-golf-clash/faq/1285-why-did-my-shot-not-land-where-i-expected-1613730444).
- Golden Tee Mobile: the putting view switches to a cutaway of the green showing slope for each putt. Wind matters much less on putts, and putts cannot be curved. Curve shots work only from the fairway or tee; other terrain hampers curve — [GTM How to Play](https://gtm-help.itsgames.com/gtm-help/help/howtoplay).
- Clap Hanz Golf: the putter has range presets (close, medium, far) that rescale the swipe — [Pocket Gamer, 2022](https://ee.pocketgamer.com/articles/084131/clap-hanz-golf-review-having-a-ball/).
- One mini-golf developer exposes per-style putting strength adjustments so users can customise putt power. This suggests the gesture-to-power mapping for putts needs tuning or user control — [Mighty Coconut support: Three ways to play](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/article/38-three-ways-to-play-touch-to-putt-swing-to-putt-standard-play/).
- A TouchArcade commenter called close-range putting "clunky" and criticised the "typical green to red meter" for power — [TouchArcade forum](https://toucharcade.com/community/threads/309558).
- Slope grid convention from golf simulators: dots move downhill, with speed proportional to slope severity. Colour runs blue (lowest) to red (highest). The grid is a toggle — [TrackMan support: Green grid colours](https://support.trackmangolf.com/hc/en-us/articles/12894735013019-Settings-Green-Grid-Colors-In-Virtual-Golf).
- Real-golf framing: the ball falls toward the low point, and most break comes from side slope — [GolfWRX green reading](https://golfwrx.com/151128/the-ultimate-putting-program-part-4-green-reading/).

### Inferences
- For one thumb: offer **spin as a small optional ball widget** (tap, drag a dot on the ball face, then auto-close), defaulting to none. Ideally unlock it after the tutorial holes so first sessions stay simple.
- Putting is where most "feels bad" reviews come from. Use **auto-scaled putt power** (100% pull = a distance just past the hole, or range presets), add a **putt preview line** whose length shrinks with difficulty, and an **animated slope grid**. Dots flowing downhill with speed showing gradient carry the information through motion and density, not only colour, which helps colour-blind players.
- Keep putts straight-line in input (no curl). Break comes only from the slope physics.

### Gaps
- No numbers found for putt preview-line length in shipped mobile games or for grid cell size.
- WGT's and Golf Rival's specific green-grid implementations were not found.

## 4. Wind and elevation display, and how strongly wind is modelled

### Takeaway
Wind is shown as an arrow plus speed in a corner, and in competitive mobile golf it is a dominant skill factor. Players complain when wind feels inconsistent or scales the same for a chip as a drive. Better systems scale wind by shot height, loft and ball type.

### Cited Findings
- Golf Clash: wind is an arrow icon in the top-left corner. Players two-finger rotate the view to align the wind arrow — [West Games](https://west-games.com/golf-clash-controls/). A tips site calls wind "arguably the most important factor" — [oembed.org tips](https://oembed.org/blog/golf-clash-tips-and-tricks) (low authority).
- Golden Tee Mobile: the indicator shows direction and strength. Some balls reduce wind effect, higher altitude increases it, low-loft clubs reduce it, and it matters much less on putts. A Heads-Up camera shows how high obstacles are ahead — [GTM How to Play](https://gtm-help.itsgames.com/gtm-help/help/howtoplay).
- Complaints: "wind affected the ball the same way on a drive and a short chip" — [Golf Impact App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/golf-impact-real-golf-game/id1532689217). Wind was "highly inconsistent" and clean shots went in unexpected directions — [Golf King App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/golf-king-world-tour/id1463360096) (user reviews).

### Inferences
- Model wind as acceleration on the ball in flight. Displacement then grows naturally with hang time and apex, so chips drift little and drives a lot. Keep it deterministic: seeded wind per hole, constant during a shot. Show it as **arrow + number + a predicted-drift ghost on the landing marker** at easy difficulty, and the arrow alone on hard.
- Show elevation as a "+/-x m" label next to distance and fold it into "plays as" distance. No source covered mobile elevation UI (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No source quantified wind strength ranges in mobile golf (for example metres of drift per mph per club).
- No mobile-specific elevation UI sources were found.

## 5. Camera: aim cam, flight follow, landing cam, green view, skip/fast-forward, shot duration, motion sickness, portrait vs landscape

### Takeaway
Games give a behind-ball aim view, a zoom-to-aim-point or bird's-eye view to inspect the landing area, a flyover or path preview, and a fast-forward during flight. Portrait is accepted for one-handed play, but some players find it odd for golf unless the holes run vertically.

### Cited Findings
- Golden Tee Mobile camera tools: Fly-By (shortest fairway path preview), Birds-Eye (two-finger pan), Heads-Up (obstacle height), Save Last Shot replay, and **Quick Shot: tap during flight to speed up, tap again for normal speed** — [GTM How to Play](https://gtm-help.itsgames.com/gtm-help/help/howtoplay).
- Clap Hanz Golf: double-tap zooms to the aim point, and a camera button sets the zoom level behind the golfer — [Pocket Gamer, 2022](https://ee.pocketgamer.com/articles/084131/clap-hanz-golf-review-having-a-ball/).
- Golf Clash: two-finger rotation of the view — [West Games](https://west-games.com/golf-clash-controls/).
- Portrait: Golf Blitz is pitched at fans of "single handed portrait focussed games" — [TouchArcade 2019](https://toucharcade.com/2019/05/01/golf-blitz-free-download-worldwide/). Pro Feel Golf advertises one finger, two actions and "convenient portrait gameplay" — [search summary, TouchArcade thread](https://toucharcade.com/community/threads/254536). A forum commenter found portrait an odd fit for golf "unless the levels are very vertical" — [TouchArcade community thread](https://toucharcade.com/community/threads/413968).
- Reduce-motion guidance: give an option to turn off or hide background movement and to reduce motion — [Game Accessibility Guidelines](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/basic/).

### Inferences
- Portrait suits golf if the **hole runs up the screen**: tee at the bottom, flag at the top. Use a high 3/4 or overhead aim view while aiming, a gentle follow during flight, and a landing hold of about 0.5-1 s. Then cut to the next shot, with a top-down green view for putts.
- Provide **tap to fast-forward** (2-4x) during flight and roll, plus a skip-replay option. Golden Tee's Quick Shot is the clearest precedent. For motion comfort, avoid fast camera rolls and FOV pumping, and offer a "reduce camera motion" setting that uses cuts or a fixed high camera instead of follow.
- Only one game in this research (Golf Clash) needs two-finger gestures (rotate). Avoid them for strict one-thumb play and put any view toggle on a single button.

### Gaps
- No published measurements of shot duration (aim + flight + roll seconds) or of turn timers in seconds were found.
- No motion-sickness reports specific to mobile golf cameras surfaced.

## 6. Session design: round and match formats, duration

### Takeaway
Leading mobile titles favour short formats: fast 1v1 hole-by-hole duels, 3/6/9-hole local matches, 9-hole score attack, single-hole tournaments, and simultaneous short races. Speed of matches is cited as a reason for popularity.

### Cited Findings
- Golf Clash's popularity is linked to "the speed of the matches", with fast 1v1 play focused on winning each hole rather than a long solo round — [Pocket Gamer: three things to know](https://www.pocketgamer.com/golf-clash/golf-clash-three-things-to-know-about-the-incredibly-popular-golf-game). It also runs 9-hole cup tournaments — [Playdemic FAQ: 9-hole cup tournaments](https://playdemic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-golf-clash/faq/1144-what-are-9-hole-cup-tournaments).
- Clap Hanz Golf: local multiplayer at 3, 6 or 9 holes, and Score Attack on 9-hole courses — [Pocket Gamer, 2022](https://ee.pocketgamer.com/articles/084131/clap-hanz-golf-review-having-a-ball/).
- PGA Tour Golf Shootout: multiplayer single-hole tournaments, a "dive-in-and-out" experience — [Pocket Gamer, 2019](https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/080252/pga-golf-tour-shootout-review-walks-the-fine-line-between-sim-and-arcade/).
- Golf Blitz: four players shoot simultaneously, and the first to fill a win counter wins. Levels are small, so respawns are quick — [Pocket Gamer, 2019](https://www.pocketgamer.com/golf-blitz/golf-blitz-review-some-of-the-best-multiplayer-that-mobile-gaming-has-to-offer/).
- Real-golf reference: an 18-hole weekday round averages about 4 hours (USGA, cited by [The Golf News Net](https://thegolfnewsnet.com/ryan_ballengee/2025/12/11/how-much-time-play-18-holes-9-holes-golf-74399/)). This is irrelevant to pacing except as a reason to compress.

### Inferences
- For an offline vs-CPU hub game, a **3-hole match-play duel (best of 3 holes)** fits a 2-4 minute session and matches the hub's existing best-of-3 pattern. Also offer **9-hole stroke play** as the long mode and a **closest-to-pin** mini mode. The CPU should play quickly, with skippable CPU shots.
- IP: avoid mode names associated with competitors (for example "Clash", "Blitz", "Shootout", "Tour 10" style naming).

### Gaps
- No source gave measured match durations (minutes) for any mobile golf title.

## 7. Accessibility: one-handed, left-handed, reduce motion, colour-blind hazards, haptics

### Takeaway
Platform and accessibility guidelines give clear numbers and rules: 48dp targets with 8dp spacing, no information by colour alone, toggles for haptics, reduced-motion options and simplified control schemes. No golf-specific accessibility findings surfaced.

### Cited Findings
- Interactive elements should be at least 48x48dp — [Android developers: Make apps more accessible](https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/ui/accessibility/apps) — separated by 8dp or more — [Google Accessibility help: Touch target size](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/7101858). Accessibility Scanner flags targets under 48dp, or under 32dp at the display edge — [Deque rule](https://dequeuniversity.com/rules/attest-android/1.0/touch-target-size).
- Unity's guidance: at least 15 mm square on phones and 24 mm on tablets — [Unity accessibility basics](https://unity.com/blog/video-game-accessibility-basics).
- Make virtual controls large and well spaced. Do not convey essential information by colour alone. Allow turning off or hiding background movement — [Game Accessibility Guidelines (basic)](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/basic/).
- Xbox Accessibility Guideline 107: offer simplified control schemes and fewer touch controls, and avoid prolonged holds, dragging and swiping, rapid taps and multi-point gestures, or provide alternatives — [Xbox accessibility guideline 107](https://devdocs.xbox.com/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/107).
- Provide a toggle or slider for haptics, and use haptics as extra feedback alongside audio and visuals — [search summary of GAG / RNIB 2025](https://media.rnib.org.uk/documents/XR_accessibility_insights_and_toolkit_Gaming_sector_report_RNIB_2025.pdf). Flashing above 3 per second is a seizure risk and needs a disable option — [secondary skill file](https://claudskills.com/skills/game-accessibility-engineer/SKILL.md) (low authority; it matches WCAG's three-flash rule, but that was not fetched).
- Golf Clash vibrates the screen when overpowering, an example of haptic or shake feedback marking a risk zone — [West Games accuracy](https://west-games.com/golf-clash-accuracy/).

### Inferences
- Drag-to-shoot conflicts with the Xbox advice to avoid dragging. A **tap-tap alternative** (tap to start the meter, tap to set power, optional tap for accuracy) is a cheap accessibility mode with genre precedent (Super Stickman Golf).
- Left-handed: mirror the bottom HUD (club button, spin button). Make the shot gesture work anywhere in the lower half, not anchored to one side as the right-side swipe in Clap Hanz Golf is.
- Hazards: give water, sand and out-of-bounds distinct texture or pattern (ripples, stipple, stakes) and icons on the landing marker, not just blue, beige and white. Make the slope grid motion-coded. Add a Reduce Motion setting covering camera follow, screen shake and slow-mo, plus a haptics slider.

### Gaps
- No golf-specific reviews about left-handed or one-handed issues were found.

## 8. Feedback and juice: trails, slow-mo near the cup, banners, sound

### Takeaway
Little citable material surfaced on juice specifically. What exists: perfect-power confirmation (flag tint), overpower screen shake, shot-feedback readouts and replays.

### Cited Findings
- After a perfect-power shot, the hole's flag rises and takes a bluish tint — [search summary, Golf Clash guide](https://www.levelwinner.com/golf-clash-ultimate-guide-13-tips-tricks-become-best-player/).
- Overpowering triggers screen vibration and an erratic needle — [West Games accuracy](https://west-games.com/golf-clash-accuracy/).
- One guide says there is no sound cue when the needle passes perfect, a missed audio-feedback opportunity — [search summary, West Games](https://west-games.com/?p=15074).
- Golden Tee shows post-shot power and gesture direction (Shot Feedback) and lets you save a replay — [GTM How to Play](https://gtm-help.itsgames.com/gtm-help/help/howtoplay).
- Reviewers praise "clear UI" and "slick and clear" menus as part of what makes these games feel good — [Pocket Gamer 2022](https://ee.pocketgamer.com/articles/084131/clap-hanz-golf-review-having-a-ball/); [Pocket Gamer 2019](https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/080252/pga-golf-tour-shootout-review-walks-the-fine-line-between-sim-and-arcade/).

### Inferences
- Recommended juice: ball trail coloured by shot grade, a soft tick sound and light haptic when timing passes the sweet spot (filling the gap above), and brief slow-mo (about 0.3-0.5x for under 1 s) when the ball rolls within about 1 m of the cup, disabled under Reduce Motion. Add a score-name banner (Birdie, Eagle) and a short post-shot readout (carry, curve, grade). These are design suggestions, not sourced numbers.

### Gaps
- No GDC talks or postmortems on mobile golf feel were found within the search budget, and no data on slow-mo timing or banner duration.
