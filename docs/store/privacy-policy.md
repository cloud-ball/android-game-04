# Privacy Policy: Pocket Arena 8

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

Pocket Arena 8 ("the app") is a collection of single-player games played against the computer.

## What we collect
Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. It has no
accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not request internet access.

## What is stored on your device
The app saves your game progress (scores, coins, settings, daily-challenge status) in a file on
your own device so you can continue where you left off. This file never leaves your device and is
deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Permissions
- **Vibrate**: optional haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes
If a future version changes any of this (for example by adding optional ads), this policy will be
updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
